The equation

Substituted with your current values.

  • 48 images total — angles × dithers × frames × cycles
  • 28.0 s per image at the detector, plus 13.0 s to read, write and step the plate

Elapsed time is tao·(ndither+1) for AO recovery at each dither, plus nangles·(trw+thwp)·ndither·nframes·ncycles to read, write and step the plate for every image, plus nangles·ndither·nframes·ncycles·coadds·(itime+tread·(nread−1)) at the detector. Efficiency is on-sky integration divided by elapsed time. All figures are estimates for planning — they exclude acquisition, focus, and calibration.
