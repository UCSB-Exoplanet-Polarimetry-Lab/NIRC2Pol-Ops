Working

Eq. 10 evaluated at the current inputs.

SNR = √N_exp · (F·t) / √( R²n + D·n·t + B·G·n·t + K·G·n·t + F·t )
  • t — itime; N_exp — nang·nd·nf·nc·coadds, so N_exp·t is exactly the on-sky integration
  • F — source rate 100.4(Z₀′−M), e⁻/s
  • R, D, B, K — read noise, dark current, sky background, residual starlight
  • n — aperture area in px; G — gain, 4 e⁻/count

Model notes

Implemented as printed in the paper. Where it is internally inconsistent or under-specified, the behaviour is reproduced and named here rather than silently corrected.

  1. Timing model. Overheads come from the NIRC2-Pol timing model used by the HWP cycle calculator, not from the 2021 paper's port of the generic Keck NIRC2 calculator. It is HWP-aware and current.
  2. Gain double-conversion. Eq. 10 multiplies Nresid by the gain to convert counts to electrons, but Nresid descends from a quantity Eq. 2 already gives in e⁻/s, so the term is inflated by G = 4. The checkbox turns it off entirely.
  3. Radial, not areal, normalization. The 2(β−1)/α² prefactor normalizes ∫M(r) dr = 1, not ∫M(r) 2πr dr = 1. That fixes α = (β−1)√π Γ(β−½)/Γ(β), so β is the only free shape parameter.
  4. Separation is an implicit input. Nresid depends on r₀, which Sec. 2 omits from its list of ETC inputs.
  5. Mixed angular units. Eq. 6 measures r in λ/D but gives the default annulus width as 10 px. Both are entered in λ/D here, with the pixel equivalent shown.
  6. No Medium camera. Table 2 lists only Narrow and Wide.
  7. No instrumental polarization or polarimetric efficiency. Excluded by Sec. 2.3 as ~0.1% and uncharacterized. SNRpol is simply SNR × p.
  8. No confidence intervals. Sec. 2 states the ETC has no way to estimate them.

The signal-to-noise model implements Nguyen, M. M., Jensen-Clem, R., Millar-Blanchaer, M. A., Mukherjee, S., Skemer, A. & Wang, J. 2021, “An exposure time calculator for high-contrast polarimeters,” Proc. SPIE 11823, 118231X, doi:10.1117/12.2597978. Instrument constants are that paper's Table 2 and preset photometry comes from the authors' reference implementation, meiji274/HCI-ETC. Overheads use this repository's own NIRC2-Pol timing model.

Eq. 10 reduces exactly to the official Keck NIRC2 SNR formula once the dark current and residual starlight terms are zeroed — see the validation page.
