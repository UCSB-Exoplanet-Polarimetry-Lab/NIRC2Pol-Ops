NIRC2-Pol Operations

NIRC2-Pol is a dual-channel polarimetry mode on the Keck II NIRC2 infrared imager. A polarizing beamsplitter separates the incoming light into two orthogonal polarization states, and a half-wave plate modulates the angle of polarization through cycles of four critical angles to recover the linear Stokes components Q and U. These are the web tools published from the operations software repository.
